## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

Not published yet.

- Decision Maker on macOS, Linux, Windows and the web, through Google's
  official MediaPipe 1.1.0 runtimes: the C library from Google's Python
  wheels on the desktop and `@mediapipe/tasks-decision` in browsers.
- `evaluateBoolean`, `evaluateChoice` and `evaluateScore` with Google's
  `BooleanQuestion`, `ChoiceQuestion` and `ScoreQuestion`, plus their batch
  forms, with the same results on every platform.
- `DecisionModels` pins Google's Laya and GLiNER2.5-Decide models.
